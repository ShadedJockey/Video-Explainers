# Style prompt: text-led archival collage (vertical)

*Distilled from three reference shorts studied frame by frame (80–115 s each). Measurements in brackets come from the references. Add your own notes in the last section.*

---

## The prompt

> Create a **vertical (9:16) short-form explainer** in a **text-led archival collage** style. A calm narrator reads a short essay about myth and psychology. Every word of the narration appears on screen as **black serif text on white paper strips**, stacked in a left-aligned column. Behind the text, a **dense, full-bleed collage of public-domain artwork keeps building**: cut-out fragments of old paintings, manuscripts, woodcuts, tarot cards, masks and colour swatches slide in from the edges and pile up, roughly one new piece per second. The look is a scholar's scrapbook or zine: flat, tactile, intelligent and quietly hypnotic. There are no stickers, no drop shadows, no bouncing and no flashy transitions. The text carries the argument and the collage carries the mood.

---

## 1. Format and rhythm

| | |
|---|---|
| Aspect | 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps |
| Length | 80–115 s (about 200–280 spoken words) |
| Hard cuts | almost none [about 4 per video: title → collage, end card]. The video is one continuous, evolving collage. |
| Collage beat | a new piece lands about every **0.9 s** on average, in irregular clusters: 2–4 pieces in quick succession, then a 1–3 s pause [44 change events in 40 s] |
| Text beat | one line every 1–2 s, locked to the narration |

## 2. Structure (script template)

1. **Title card (0–3 s).** A single striking full-bleed image or a short footage clip tied to the topic (a painting of the myth, or animal footage). On it sits a white box with the title in a large condensed serif across two lines, **last word in italics**, and an italic subtitle underneath ("[Thinker] on [idea]").
   - Title formulas: *"Why [myth pattern] …"* or *"From [X] to [Y]"*.
2. **Myth evidence (about 25 %).** Three or four quick examples from different cultures, one or two lines each, with the **name in bold**: "In Polynesia, **Maui** tricks the fire goddess out of her flames."
3. **The pattern (one line).** "Almost nobody in myth is simply **given** fire."
4. **The thinker's reading (about 30 %).** What the theorist noticed, including one short direct quote with "he wrote."
5. **Deepening (about 20 %).** A second idea or book that takes it further, often a paradox.
6. **Turn to the viewer (about 10 %).** "So if … has made you feel …, the myth has a place for that."
7. **Closing aphorism.** One line set entirely in *italics*: "*Waking up has always felt a little like theft.*"
8. **Outro (last 4–6 s).** The text holds and the series tag switches to a black tag with the website or handle in white.

**Writing voice:** short declarative sentences, concrete nouns, no hype words, no questions to camera except at the turn. One **bold keyword per sentence** (a name, the key concept or the twist word).

## 3. Typography

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Body font | Old-style Garamond serif (e.g. **EB Garamond** Regular plus Bold for keywords) |
| Title font | Condensed high-contrast serif (e.g. **Instrument Serif** or Playfair Display Condensed), with the last word in its italic |
| Body size | about 50 px at 1080 wide; line pitch about 0.05 × frame height |
| Line length | at most about 32 characters per line; break lines at natural phrase boundaries |
| Text strips | **each line has its own opaque white rectangle** that hugs the text (about 0.03 W padding left and right, about 0.004 H gap between strips). Strip widths vary with the line, which creates a ragged right edge. |
| Column | left-aligned, starting about 0.19 H from the top. Strips start at x = 0–0.04 W, and each new paragraph shifts its indent by about ±0.02 W (like separately pasted strips). |
| Column limit | when the stack reaches about 0.75 H, the whole column **scrolls up smoothly** (about 0.3 s) and the oldest lines slide under the header tags and off the top |
| Header tags | two small white tags at about 0.11–0.17 H: a **series name** flush to the left edge (bold) and a **topic tag** at about 0.45 W (regular). They stay on top of everything. At the end the topic tag becomes a **black tag with white text** (website or handle). |
| Title card box | white, from the left edge to about 0.65 W, at about 0.51–0.76 H; title cap height about 0.075 H; subtitle italic at about 0.022 H |
| Text colour | near-black (#111) on white (#fff). No outlines, no shadows. |

**Text animation:** each new line **wipes in left to right in about 0.15–0.25 s**, timed to the narrator starting that phrase. There's no typewriter effect, no bounce and no fade-out; lines only leave by scrolling up.

## 4. The collage

- **Coverage:** 100 % full-bleed at all times; no plain background is ever visible. It starts from the title image, and pieces accumulate on top of it.
- **Pieces:** sized 25–70 % of the frame width. About half are straight rectangular crops; the rest are **rough scissor cuts** (irregular polygons with 5–9 straight, slightly jagged edges), with the occasional torn edge.
- **Flat:** **no white sticker borders and no drop shadows** (at most a 1–2 px hairline or an extremely faint shadow). Rotation 0–4°.
- **Entrances:** each piece **slides in from the nearest frame edge** over 0.2–0.5 s with a strong ease-out (cubic or quint). There's **no overshoot**, and some pieces simply cut in. Pieces never leave; new ones cover old ones. Occasionally a piece slides across and parks over the text area *behind* the strips.
- **Layering:** the collage continues behind the text column. The white strips sit above it, so it shows through the gaps and ragged edges. The header tags sit above everything.
- **Motion:** no camera moves, zooms or wobble. The life comes from new pieces arriving and from the text. Optionally, one or two pieces per video are **short looping video clips** (archival animal footage, a flickering flame) inside a cut-out shape.
- **Relevance:** roughly every third piece should echo the current line (fire, a trickster, a child, a wolf). The rest are texture: patterns, colour fields and old print.

### Image palette (public domain only)

- Renaissance and Baroque oil paintings (figures, hands, faces, drapery)
- Medieval manuscript illuminations, marginalia and grotesques
- Alchemical and emblem-book woodcuts, astrological diagrams, sun and moon faces
- Tarot cards (Marseille-style), playing cards, jesters
- Ethnographic masks, Greek vase figures, folk art, ukiyo-e prints
- Natural-history engravings, old animal photographs
- Fragments of old book pages (blackletter, Latin, footnotes and captions)
- Flat colour paper swatches and patterns: checkerboard, op-art, marbled paper

**Colour mood:** muted and warm. Maroon (#6b2a2e), oxblood, ochre and mustard, dusty pink, slate blue, sage, cream and ink black, with small pops of saturated red. Avoid clean modern colours.

## 5. Audio

- **Narrator:** calm, intimate and unhurried, like reading aloud in a quiet room, at about 150–165 wpm. Natural pauses at line breaks and slightly longer pauses between paragraphs.
- **Bed:** soft ambient or minimal piano/drone, low (about −25 dB under the voice), with no beat drops.
- **Effects:** none or almost none. Optionally a very quiet paper slide or rustle on some piece entrances.
- **Loudness:** −14 to −16 LUFS integrated for short-form platforms.

## 6. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Let the text carry the argument: every spoken word on screen | Add separate "caption" stamps, emojis or kinetic typography |
| Keep the collage dense, flat and always growing | Leave empty paper, use white sticker borders or shadows |
| Use fast ease-out slides without overshoot | Bounce, wobble, pop, spin or zoom |
| Bold one word per sentence | Bold whole phrases or use colour for emphasis |
| End on an italic one-line aphorism | End on a hard sell or a "like and subscribe" voice line |
| Credit artwork sources in the description | Copy another channel's series name, branding or website tag |

## 7. Technical recipe (for our pipeline)

- **Canvas:** 1080×1920. Layer order, bottom to top: collage pieces (accumulating) → text strips → header tags.
- **Piece schedule:** generate arrival times as clusters (Poisson, mean gap 0.9 s, cluster size 1–4). For each piece pick an edge, a final position (biased so that coverage stays 100 %), a size of 0.25–0.7 W and a cut style (rectangle 50 %, polygon 40 %, torn 10 %). Animate it from off-frame to its position with ease-out-quint over 0.2–0.5 s.
- **Cut shapes:** polygons from 5–9 vertices jittered around the crop rectangle, with straight edges and no feathering. Torn edges use a noise-displaced edge with a 2–3 px light paper fringe.
- **Text:** split the narration into lines of at most 32 characters. Each line's start time is the narration timestamp, so word timings come from TTS or forced alignment. A strip's width is the measured text width plus padding. Reveal is a left-to-right clip mask over 0.2 s. Scroll the column when its bottom exceeds 0.75 H.
- **Fonts:** EB Garamond (body), Instrument Serif (title). Both are free on Google Fonts.
- **Export:** H.264, CRF about 20, 30 fps, AAC 192 kbps.

## 8. Differences from the current Hero Archetypes build

| Current build | This style |
|---|---|
| 16:9, sparse paper background with a few large stickers | 9:16, dense full-bleed collage that builds continuously |
| White sticker borders, drop shadows, wobble, overshoot pops | Flat cut-outs, fast ease-out slides, no wobble |
| Narration heard; short stamp captions on screen | Narration shown in full as a scrolling text column on white strips |
| Display fonts (Anton, marker, typewriter) | Garamond serif body plus condensed serif title |
| Paper-tear transitions between scenes | One continuous collage with almost no cuts |
