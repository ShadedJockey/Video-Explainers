Audition text · draft 4, opening lines People have told hero stories for as long as they have told stories. One of the oldest, the Epic of Gilgamesh, was written in Mesopotamia about four thousand years ago. In 1949, the American mythologist Joseph Campbell published The Hero with a Thousand Faces. By faces, he meant people: each hero is another instance of the same figure. Biology has a name for this kind of pattern: convergent evolution. The philosopher Karl Popper argued that knowledge grows by finding and correcting errors.
