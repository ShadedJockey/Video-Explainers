- `0:20.0` Campbell called it the **monomyth**. The hero leaves home, is tested, and returns changed.

- `0:37.3` Folklorists pointed out that he chose the stories that fit, and quietly left out the ones that didn't.
- `0:43.4` Real heroes skip stages, repeat them, fail them, and invent new ones.
- `0:48.3` The wise mentor, the magic sword, the guardian at the gate. These are **local** details. Costumes.
